Questions

Before you give.

Is my payment secure?

Yes. Payments are handled entirely by Pesapal, a licensed payment gateway used across East Africa. AFOSI never receives or stores your card number or M-Pesa PIN.

What payment methods can I use?

M-Pesa (an STK push prompt is sent to your phone) or any Visa/Mastercard debit or credit card.

Will I get a receipt?

Yes — Pesapal sends a confirmation immediately after payment. For any additional documentation, email info@afosi.org with your reference number.

Can I cancel a monthly donation?

Any time — email info@afosi.org with the name or phone/email you donated with and we'll stop it right away.

Where exactly does my money go?

Directly into AFOSI's active programs across health, education, environment, livelihoods, leadership and humanitarian response. For a fuller picture of our work, see About and Projects.
