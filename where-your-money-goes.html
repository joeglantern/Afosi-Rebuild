How it works

From your card or phone to a community programme.

01

You give, Paystack processes it

Your M-Pesa or card payment is handled entirely by Paystack. AFOSI never sees or stores your card number or M-Pesa PIN, and you get an instant receipt.

02

Funds go into active programmes

Your gift is pooled with other donations and applied to AFOSI's current work, there is no separate fundraising layer taking a cut before it reaches a programme.

03

Our teams and platforms deliver it

Field teams run the on-the-ground work; our own digital platforms (Kiongozi, Kenya Youth Climate Hub, Afosi Hub) extend its reach and let us track and report on outcomes.

Programme areas

What your donation actually funds.

Donations are not earmarked to a single project unless a donor requests it, they go into a shared pool that keeps these programmes running.

SRHR & LEADERSHIP

We Lead Project

Peer-led SRHR education, leadership training and support for young women living with HIV and disabilities, placing them at the centre of change.

STEM EDUCATION

Robotics & Creative Coding

With STEM Impact Center Kenya, hands-on robotics and coding labs for youth in informal settlements, opening pathways into STEM careers.

JUSTICE & LIVELIHOODS

Sheria ya Vijana

Empowers youth in Nairobi and Kwale through skills, leadership and participation in the green and digital economy.

HEALTH & ENVIRONMENT

M.A.T.H

Mazingira, Afya, Tumaini na Haki yetu, a three-year initiative running in 60 APBET schools across Kibera and Mukuru.

ADVOCACY

Youth Voices Lab

AI-supported digital advocacy giving voice to young women living with HIV and with disabilities in Mukuru.

ENTERPRISE

YOMA Projects

A digital marketplace giving youth a unique digital identity so they can learn, earn and impact their communities.

These initiatives run through our own digital platforms, the Kiongozi Platform, Kenya Youth Climate Hub and Afosi Hub, which keep delivery costs low and outcomes trackable. See the full breakdown on Programs.

How we keep it accountable

  • Every donation is processed and receipted by Paystack, a licensed payment gateway, before it touches AFOSI's account.
  • Our board oversees the organisation's finances and programme spending under AFOSI's constitution.
  • Programmes are run by AFOSI staff and named local partners (e.g. STEM Impact Center Kenya), not third-party fundraisers.
  • Monthly gifts can be cancelled at any time, no questions asked.
